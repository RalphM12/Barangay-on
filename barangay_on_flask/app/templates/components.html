Privacy & data use

Barangay-On keeps only the information needed to run barangay services: your account name, your resident record (name, address, contact number) and the requests you file.

  • Only authorized barangay staff can see resident records.
  • Your password is stored in a protected (hashed) form and is never shown to anyone.
  • Your display preferences in Settings are private to your account.

To correct your records or ask for your account to be removed, please contact the barangay office or the system administrator.

Help & support

Need help using the portal? Visit or call the barangay hall during office hours, or ask the system administrator. The hotlines and office hours are listed on the home page.

Accessibility: every control can be used with the keyboard (Tab, Enter, Space, Esc). Use Appearance to switch to dark mode or a larger font size.
